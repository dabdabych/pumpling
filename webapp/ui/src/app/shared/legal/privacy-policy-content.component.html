1. Who we are

Pumpling is an independent, early-stage project operating the website at pumpling.xyz and the related platform (the “Platform”). Pumpling is not a registered company. The Platform is provided by the project team on an as-is basis.

For any question about this policy, or about the data we hold, write to Pumpling.xyz@gmail.com. We read that inbox and we answer it.

2. What this policy covers

This policy explains what personal data we collect, why, who else can see it, and what you can ask us to do about it.

It does not cover the Solana blockchain, your wallet provider, or any third-party website you reach from ours. Those are outside our control.

3. Data you give us

If you register with an email address, we store:

  • your email address;
  • the nickname you choose;
  • a bcrypt hash of your password — we never store the password itself, and we cannot recover it;
  • a hashed, time-limited token when you confirm your email or reset your password;
  • whether your account is active and whether your email is verified;
  • the dates your account was created and last updated.

If you connect a Solana wallet, we store your public wallet address and a short-lived one-time value (“nonce”) used to prove you control that wallet.

We never ask for, receive, or store private keys or seed phrases. Nobody from Pumpling will ever ask you for them. If someone does, it is not us.

If you write to us, we keep your message for as long as we need it to answer you.

4. Data created by using the Platform

When you take part in a round we record the round, the memecoin you chose, the amount of SOL, your wallet address, and the transaction signature.

Please read this part before you register. If you use both an email account and a wallet, our records connect the two. Your email address is therefore linked to your wallet address and to everything you have done on the Platform. Because blockchain activity is public and permanent, anyone who obtained that link could trace your on-chain history back to you.

We do not sell that link and we do not publish it. But it exists, and you should decide with that in mind. If you want to keep your on-chain activity separate from your identity, use a wallet you do not use anywhere else — or use the Platform with a wallet only, without registering an email.

5. The public chat

The chat on pumpling.xyz is public. Anyone can read it, including people without an account, and anyone who sees a message can copy or save it.

To post you need an account. When you post, we store the message, any image you attach, the message you replied to, the time, and your account ID. Other people see your nickname next to your messages, not your email address.

If you signed in with a wallet only, your nickname is made from your wallet address, so the start of that address is shown next to your messages. Never post seed phrases, private keys, or anything you would not want to be public.

We keep messages and images for as long as the chat runs. You cannot delete a message yourself yet: write to us and we will remove it. We may remove messages and restrict accounts that spam, scam, or break the law. To limit spam we count how often each account posts; that count lives only briefly in server memory.

6. Data collected automatically

Our servers and hosting provider process the technical data needed to serve the site: IP address, browser type, and request timestamps.

Solana RPC providers we use to read the chain and submit transactions also see requests coming from your session.

7. Analytics

We use Google Analytics 4 to understand how many people visit pumpling.xyz and which parts of it they use. We use it to improve the product — not to profile you, not to advertise to you, and not to sell anything about you.

Analytics loads when you open the site. We do not currently show a consent prompt, and we are telling you that plainly rather than describing a choice you were not given. If you would rather not be counted, Google publishes a browser opt-out add-on that blocks Google Analytics everywhere: tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout. Browser tracking protection and content blockers stop it as well.

Google Analytics may:

  • set identifiers in your browser to recognise repeat visits;
  • record which pages you open, when, and for how long;
  • record your device and browser type;
  • record your approximate location — country, and usually city.

About IP addresses. Like any request your browser makes, the request to Google carries your IP address at the network level. Google uses it to work out roughly where you are and, according to Google’s documentation for Google Analytics 4, does not store it afterwards. We never receive or see IP addresses through Analytics — the Analytics interface shows us approximate location only, never an address.

This is separate from our own server logs, described in section 6, which do record IP addresses in order to serve the site and keep it secure.

This data is processed by Google (Google Ireland Limited for users in the European Economic Area, and Google LLC), and may be transferred outside the EEA under the safeguards Google has in place. Google’s own privacy policy governs what it does with the data: policies.google.com/privacy.

We have set the shortest data retention period Google offers, and we do not enable Google’s advertising or cross-device features.

We do not use any other analytics, advertising networks, or third-party trackers.

9. Emails we send

We send transactional email only: confirming your address, resetting your password, and messages about your account or a round you took part in.

We do not send marketing email, and we do not share your address with anyone for marketing. If that ever changes we will ask you first, separately.

Email is delivered through Brevo (Sendinblue SAS, France), which processes your address in order to deliver the message. Brevo is subject to its own privacy terms.

10. Who else can see your data

  • The blockchain. Your wallet address, your transactions and the coins you bought are public and permanent. We cannot delete, hide, or edit them. This is how public blockchains work, and it applies whether or not you ever registered with us.
  • Our providers: hosting, Solana RPC, Brevo for email, and Google for analytics. Each sees only what it needs to do its job.
  • Authorities, if we are legally required to disclose something.

We do not sell your personal data.

11. How long we keep it

We keep account data while your account exists. On request we delete your email address, nickname, and password hash.

Records of on-chain participation may remain in anonymised form, and the underlying blockchain transactions remain public regardless of anything we do.

Server logs are kept for a short period for security and debugging. Analytics data is kept for the shortest period Google Analytics allows.

12. Your rights

If you are in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom you have the right to access your data, correct it, delete it, restrict or object to how we use it, and receive a copy in a portable format.

Write to Pumpling.xyz@gmail.com and we will act within one month. Account deletion is currently handled manually by the team on request.

You also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority.

13. Security

Passwords are stored as bcrypt hashes with individual salts. Email confirmation and password-reset tokens are stored as hashes and expire. Traffic to the Platform is encrypted in transit.

No system is perfectly secure, and we do not claim otherwise. Use a password you do not reuse elsewhere.

14. International transfers

Some providers we rely on operate outside the European Economic Area. Where that happens, transfers rest on the safeguards those providers have in place, such as the European Commission’s standard contractual clauses or an applicable adequacy decision.

15. Children

The Platform is not intended for anyone under 18, and we do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a minor has registered, write to us and we will remove the account.
