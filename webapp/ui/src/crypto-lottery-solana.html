How the Solana lottery works

Each round moves through three transparent phases:

  1. Prediction.

    Connect your Solana wallet and back a memecoin with SOL — your own coin or one already in the round. The minimum entry is 0.05 SOL. More SOL on a coin means a higher chance it wins. The phase stays open for a set window, or ends early once the prize pool hits its cap.

  2. Draw.

    Deposits close. Our smart contract pulls a verifiable on-chain random number to select the winning coins. This takes about 1–3 minutes and the result is public.

  3. Execution.

    A 3% platform fee is deducted; the remaining 97% becomes the buy budget. The system buys the winning coins on the market in smaller batches (so one large buy doesn't spike the price) and sends the tokens straight to backers' wallets — proportionally to their stake. You don't do anything; tokens arrive automatically.

Two independent lotteries: PUMP and DEX

QRES runs two separate Solana memecoin lotteries. PUMP is for coins created on Pump.fun. DEX is for Solana coins traded on decentralized exchanges (Raydium, Orca, PumpSwap, and others). They run independently — different participants, timing, and outcomes. Enter one or both.

Why QRES is provably fair

Winner selection uses an on-chain random number, not a hidden server-side roll. Our VRF supporting script and IDL are public, so anyone can verify how winners are picked. The platform is non-custodial — QRES never holds your keys or funds; you interact directly with Solana smart contracts from your own wallet. Every bet and result is visible to everyone, live.

Frequently asked questions

What is a crypto lottery on Solana?

A round-based game where you commit SOL to back a memecoin. If it's selected as a winner by an on-chain random draw, the prize pool buys that coin and distributes the tokens to its backers proportionally to their SOL.

What do I need to participate?

A Solana wallet and SOL. Connect, pick a coin, place SOL. Minimum entry is 0.05 SOL.

Is it provably fair?

Yes. Winners are picked with a verifiable on-chain random number, and our VRF script and IDL are public.

What are the fees?

A 3% platform fee on the budget; the remaining 97% buys the winning coins. Standard Solana network fees also apply. No additional fees.

Can more than one coin win?

Yes — several coins can win in one draw, depending on how SOL is distributed across coins.

How do I receive my tokens?

Automatically, straight to your wallet. If you don't have a token account yet, it's created for you during distribution.

Is QRES affiliated with Pump.fun or a DEX?

No. These are third-party infrastructure used only to execute purchases of winning coins.
